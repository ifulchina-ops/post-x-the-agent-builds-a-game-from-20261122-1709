# The Prompt

Build a tiny one-button arcade game: a neon rocket dodges falling meteors.
Hold mouse, touch, or Space to thrust up — release to fall. Survive as long
as you can: the score climbs every second and the meteors get faster. One
hit ends the run. Show the score, the best score, and a restart button.
